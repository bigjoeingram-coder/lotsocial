## Money or identity information at risk

Contact the payment provider or financial institution immediately. LotSocial cannot reverse a payment, recover an account, or replace an official fraud report. Use the [Fraud Awareness steps](/legal/fraud-awareness) and the official reporting channels listed there.

## Preserve the evidence

- The full URL of the social post or page where you saw it.
- The dealership vehicle-page URL, if shown.
- Vehicle year, make, model, VIN, or stock number when available.
- A screenshot showing the issue, date, time, account name, and caption.
- A short explanation of what appears wrong and how you verified it.
- A safe contact method for follow-up. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, bank details, or unnecessary identity documents.

## Use a verified channel

If you are a LotSocial pilot user, use the verified business or support channel that issued your access. If you are a consumer, contact the dealership through the main phone number or contact page on its official website and ask it to preserve the post and current vehicle record. Do not trust a contact method supplied only by the questioned social account.

LotSocial does not publish an unverified reporting address in this v0.9 policy. That avoids directing sensitive reports to a guessed or impersonated destination.

## Report format

Include the post or page URL, dealership vehicle URL, vehicle identifiers, issue type, what appears wrong, how you verified it, the observation date and time, and a safe method for follow-up.

## What the recipient should do

The recipient should preserve the report, compare the post with the captured source and current dealership page, check the authorization record, and pause or correct affected content when warranted. A report does not promise a particular outcome or response time.

Suspected fraud can also be reported at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/). Identity theft can be reported at [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/).
