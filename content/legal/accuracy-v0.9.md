## What LotSocial records

LotSocial reads an exact public dealership vehicle-detail-page URL and extracts vehicle facts and media from that source. The pilot records the capture time and may preserve the source copy, content hash, extracted facts, and screenshot status so a later issue can be investigated.

A source record shows what was captured at a point in time. It is not a promise that every fact was complete, correct, or still current.

## What can change or go wrong

- A vehicle may sell, move, or be removed after capture.
- Price, mandatory fees, rebates, incentives, eligibility, mileage, equipment, condition, and availability can change.
- The dealership page itself may contain an error or omit a material condition.
- Automated extraction or AI-assisted copy may misread, shorten, combine, or misstate a fact.
- Images or descriptions may be controlled by a dealer, feed provider, photographer, or another rights holder.

## Required review before publishing

1. Open the current dealership source page.
2. Match the vehicle using the VIN or stock number when available.
3. Confirm availability, total advertised price, mandatory fees, incentive eligibility, equipment, condition, and material restrictions with the dealership.
4. Compare the final caption, voiceover, images, and end card with the current source.
5. Correct or remove any conflict. If it cannot be resolved, do not publish or rely on the draft.

The seven-day caption expiration is a maximum. It never permits known inaccurate content to stay live.

## Pricing must be truthful and transparent

Dealers remain responsible for advertising in every channel, including social media. The Federal Trade Commission's [Automobile Industry Pricing Transparency FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/automobile-industry-pricing-transparency-faqs) explain that advertised prices must be truthful and transparent and address required fees and eligibility conditions. Application to a specific advertisement depends on its facts; consult counsel when needed.

## Found a conflict

Preserve the post URL, dealership source URL, vehicle identifiers, date, and screenshots. Pause the content and use the [reporting process](/legal/report).
