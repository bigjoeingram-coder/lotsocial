## Public does not mean free to reuse

A public web page is visible to everyone. That does not mean every fact, image, description, feed, trademark, or social account is free to reuse.

## Three separate permission gates

1. Dealership approval: an authorized dealership manager controls use of dealership facts, pricing, availability information, and dealership publishing activity.
2. Provider-controlled rights: a feed or media provider may control licenses or delivery rights for images, descriptions, window stickers, feeds, or other material. Manager approval does not replace those rights.
3. Publishing access: creating a caption, storyboard, or render does not authorize posting. Account credentials, platform permissions, and final dealership approval remain separate.

## What the pilot records

LotSocial can record requested permissions, the people involved, typed approval, restrictions, effective dates, decisions, status changes, and an audit history. Approval can be limited, suspended, revoked, declined, or expired. The current authorization state controls; an old screenshot or stale link does not.

## User certification

Before importing a vehicle, the user certifies that they are authorized to market the dealership's vehicle and use approved public vehicle-page content for dealership social posts. Do not certify that statement if authority is missing, uncertain, disputed, or withdrawn.

## Minimum safe behavior

- Use the exact public vehicle-detail page; do not bypass a login, paywall, access control, or technical restriction.
- Use only the permission categories actually approved.
- Honor manager restrictions, provider decisions, expiration, suspension, and revocation immediately.
- Review current vehicle facts and final creative before publishing.
- Do not imply that LotSocial approval is legal advice, an intellectual-property license, or a social-platform credential.

If authorization is disputed, stop using the material, preserve the authorization record and challenged post, and follow the [reporting process](/legal/report).
