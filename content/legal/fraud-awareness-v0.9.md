## Know LotSocial's role

LotSocial helps authorized dealership users create content from public vehicle pages. It is not a dealership, seller, lender, escrow service, payment processor, title service, inspection service, or delivery company. LotSocial does not price, inspect, own, guarantee, or accept deposits for vehicles, and it does not contact buyers to collect money.

## Stop when you see these warning signs

- A demand for gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfer, or payment to an unrelated person or account.
- Pressure to pay before you independently verify the dealership, vehicle, VIN, title, and current listing.
- A price far below the dealership's current page without a clear written explanation.
- A request for passwords, one-time codes, Social Security numbers, or bank credentials through social messages.
- A copied logo, misspelled domain, new social account, or contact detail that does not match the dealership's official website.
- A seller who refuses a live call through the dealership's publicly listed main number.

## Verify independently

1. Type the dealership website address yourself or locate it through a trusted directory.
2. Call the main number published on the dealership's official website and ask for the vehicle by VIN or stock number.
3. Ask the dealership to confirm availability and send the out-the-door price and eligibility terms in writing before you travel or pay.
4. Never send money or sensitive credentials only because a social post creates urgency.

The FTC's [Car Dealer Ads and Promotions guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/car-dealer-ads-and-promotions-know-you-go) includes additional verification steps for buyers.

## If money or identity information may be at risk

Contact the payment provider or financial institution immediately. Report suspected fraud at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/). If identity information was exposed, use [IdentityTheft.gov](https://www.identitytheft.gov/). Contact local emergency services if anyone is in immediate danger.

Also preserve the post URL, account name, messages, payment instructions, vehicle URL, and screenshots, then follow the [LotSocial reporting checklist](/legal/report).
