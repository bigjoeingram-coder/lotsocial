## Vehicle information and generated content

LotSocial may read a public dealership vehicle-detail page and preserve source evidence from the time of import. Dealer pages can change and can contain errors. Automated extraction and AI-assisted copy can also be incomplete or wrong.

Before publishing, you must review the current source page and confirm the vehicle identity, availability, total advertised price, mandatory fees, incentive eligibility, equipment, images, disclosures, and every material claim. Do not publish or leave live content that you know is inaccurate.

Read the [Listing Accuracy policy](/legal/accuracy) for the required verification standard.
