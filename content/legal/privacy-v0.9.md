## Scope

This policy describes information handled by the LotSocial pilot when it provides account access, dealership authorization, vehicle import, evidence preservation, AI-assisted content, media rendering, and pilot operations.

## Information the pilot handles

- Account and business details, such as name, work email, phone number, dealership, role, profile photo, and sign-in or invitation records.
- Authorization records, including requested permissions, manager and provider contacts, decisions, restrictions, typed approvals, status changes, and audit events.
- Vehicle-source information, including the submitted public URL, source host, capture time, extracted vehicle facts, images, source copy, content hash, and screenshot status when available.
- Creative and media information, including selected images, captions, scripts, end-card details, render requests, stored media, and download or status records.
- Security, usage, and diagnostic events needed to operate, protect, measure, and troubleshoot the pilot.

Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, payment-card data, bank credentials, Social Security numbers, or identity documents through a LotSocial caption, listing report, or ordinary support message.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide requested features, scope data to the correct account, preserve authorization and source evidence, generate and render content, maintain security, enforce limits, investigate errors, support users, and evaluate the pilot.

LotSocial does not use a vehicle post to collect a buyer deposit or payment. The pilot does not provide a consumer vehicle checkout.

## Service providers and disclosure

The pilot may send the minimum information needed to providers that perform hosting, database, storage, email, public-page retrieval, AI, rendering, analytics, security, or support functions. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

## Retention and security

Pilot information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, preserve authorization or source evidence, investigate disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the system. Retention can vary by record type. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## Choices and requests

You may stop using the pilot. For an account or pilot-data request, use the verified business or support channel that issued your LotSocial access. The operator may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request.

California residents can review the California Attorney General's [CCPA overview](https://www.oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa). Whether a particular right applies depends on the law and the facts. This policy does not claim that every privacy law applies to every pilot record.
